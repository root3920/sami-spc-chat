## What is the Secure Parent Collective (SPC)?
A parenting membership community by HIC Parenting Education. Members get weekly live coaching with Marcela Collier and the HIC coaches, a library of recorded classes and mini-series based on the U.S.E. Process, practical tools and downloads, and a community of parents walking the same journey. The goal is to become a calmer, more confident secure parent and raise emotionally secure children.

## What do members receive?
- Weekly LIVE sessions on Zoom (Thursdays). Usual flow: intentions, announcements, success stories, teaching, and live coaching; sometimes small-group "secure parent circles". Exact time and Zoom link: "Next Events" page in the members area and the reminder emails.
- Recordings: the most recent session is in "Replay of events and last session ▶️ → Last Session Replay". Full series are added as modules.
- The U.S.E. Process lessons (Aware, Growing and Leader Parent modules), the mini-series and seasons, the Library of Coaching Highlights, practical tools (breathing exercises for kids, parenting strengths and needs assessment, guided calming meditations).
- The private WhatsApp community (see below).
- Special events announced to members (for example workshops and the SPC Book Club), listed in "Next Events".

## Where do I start?
1. Watch "Welcome To SPC" (7 min) in the "Welcome to the Secure Parent Collective! 💙" module.
2. Read "Your Secure Parent Journey" and save the "Daily S.A.F.E Practice" graphic.
3. Check "Next Events" and join the next Thursday live session.
4. Start the U.S.E. Process: Aware Parent (U) → Leader Parent (S) → Growing Parent (E), or jump to the series that matches your biggest challenge (see the Content Map and the Situation Guide).

## Is this therapy? Can you diagnose my child?
No. SPC is parenting education and coaching. Sami cannot diagnose or give medical, psychological or legal advice. For health, development, diagnosis or medication questions: the child's pediatrician or a licensed professional.

## Safety (always first)
If a member mentions that a child or adult is in danger, abuse, self-harm, suicidal thoughts, or a medical emergency: tell them to contact local emergency services right away (in the US: 911; mental health crisis line: 988). Then suggest contacting the HIC team. Don't continue with parenting tips until safety is addressed.
